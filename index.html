Rocket Ship Build Guide

A compact pre-flight checklist for the AOS HS5 speed build.

01 · Prepare

  • Set the soldering iron to 350–380°C.
  • Gather flux, a multimeter, wire strippers, and foam tape.
  • Keep the smoke stopper ready for the first power-up.

02 · Frame

  • Use M3 screws and snug torque only.
  • Check motor screws do not reach the windings.
  • Plan battery leads and VTX antenna toward the rear.

03 · Power

  • Solder the XT60 directly to the main battery pads.
  • Add the 1000uF 50V low-ESR capacitor at those same pads.
  • Keep capacitor legs and battery wiring as short as practical.

04 · Wiring

  • Connect ESC signals to M1–M4 with a shared ground reference.
  • Cross ELRS receiver RX/TX lines: RX to TX, TX to RX.
  • Power a 2.5W VTX from VBAT, never the 5V rail.

05 · Configure

  • Remove props before touching the Motors tab.
  • Verify motor order and direction in Betaflight.
  • Start with conservative PIDs and restore filtering if you see jello.

06 · Flight

  • Secure every wire away from motor bells and props.
  • Use the smoke stopper, then inspect for heat or shorts.
  • Land early on the first pack and check battery voltage.
Safety first: Props off for all bench tests. A loose prop, reversed motor, short circuit, or powered VTX without an antenna can cause serious damage. Fly only in a legal, open area and disarm immediately if video is lost.

High-Speed Build Specs

The selected hardware stack for a lightweight 5-inch external-ESC racer.

Flight Stack

  • iFlight Borg F7 Mini FC with STM32F722RET6.
  • ICM42688 gyro and 32MB Blackbox.
  • Four T-Motor F60A PRO II 64-bit 6S ESCs.
  • ESC rating: 60A continuous, 70A burst, 4–8S LiHV.

Motors

  • Option 1: BrotherHobby Avenger V3 2306 2650KV.
  • Option 2: T-Motor F2004 2850KV, about 22g each.
  • Both options are extreme 6S setups; manage throttle carefully.

Analog FPV

  • DeepSpace DS 5.8G 2.5W VTX, adjustable 25–2500mW.
  • Foxeer T-Rex Micro 1200TVL or RunCam Phoenix 2.
  • 2.1mm lens with Lumenier AXII HD 2 or Foxeer Lollipop 4+.
  • Use an SMA 90° adapter only with a securely attached antenna.

Airframe & Power

  • AOS HS5 5-inch high-speed frame.
  • GNB 6S 1100mAh 130C LiHV or Tattu R-Line V5 1050mAh.
  • Gemfan 51466 or HQProp DT 5.1×4.1×3 props.
  • HappyModel EP1 ELRS 2.4GHz receiver, or 915MHz for range.

Weight Reduction

  • Direct-solder motor wires to ESC pads; avoid bullet connectors.
  • Trim ESC, receiver, and VTX wiring to the required length.
  • Skip buzzer, GPS, LEDs, and GoPro hardware when not needed.
  • Add a low-ESR 1000uF 50V capacitor at the main battery pads.

Tuning Targets

  • Starting rates: roll 900°/s and pitch 750°/s.
  • Use throttle PID attenuation to reduce full-throttle oscillation.
  • Validate motor temperature, current draw, and video range gradually.
Critical warning: Props must be removed for bench tests. Use a smoke stopper or current-limited supply before connecting a LiPo. A 2650KV motor on 6S can destroy the stack during a desync, and a high-power VTX must never be operated without its antenna connected.

Software Tuning Guide

A measured Betaflight, ESC, ELRS, and Blackbox workflow for high-speed stability.

01 · Betaflight Core

  • Flash the latest stable Betaflight 4.5.x for the F7 target.
  • Use DSHOT600 and enable Bidirectional DShot.
  • Set RPM filter slots to 2 and start Q at 400.
  • Set TPA breakpoint to 1650 and TPA rate to 0.70.

02 · Filters & PID

  • Start I-term Relax at 15–20 to reduce propwash.
  • Set Dynamic Notch to 80–600Hz with Q 1.5.
  • Enable D_Min at about 0.6 of normal D-gain.
  • Change one setting at a time and log the result.

03 · BLHeli_32 ESC

  • Update ESC firmware to 32.7 or later.
  • Use Auto motor timing; compare Low or Medium-High carefully.
  • Start startup power at 0.75–1.00 if motors stutter.
  • Try 48kHz PWM where supported and keep Demag Low.

04 · ELRS Link

  • Select 1000Hz Full packet rate where the link supports it.
  • Use telemetry ratio 1:2 or Std for racing.
  • Fix transmitter power at 100mW or higher for a solid link.
  • Disable unnecessary telemetry sensors to preserve bandwidth.

05 · Blackbox

  • Enable Blackbox and assign logging to a switch.
  • Capture full-throttle passes, rolls, and hard turns.
  • Inspect gyro noise peaks, D-term oscillation, and P-term overshoot.
  • Use Blackbox Explorer or PID Toolbox, then re-test.

06 · Optional Paths

  • Use a Betaflight 5-inch racing or UAV Tech preset as a starting point.
  • Rotorflight is an alternative, rate-based firmware for experienced pilots.
  • Keep presets experimental and verify every setting before arming.
Process and safety: Remove props for motor direction or ESC tests. Use a smoke stopper after firmware changes, monitor motor temperature after the first flight, and lower D-gain or increase TPA if motors become dangerously hot.
